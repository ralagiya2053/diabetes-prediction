# Service Policy Overview

## What does this site do?

Sample placeholder content: the site produces a data-driven estimate of
diabetes risk from eight health indicators and explains concepts related to
diabetes measurement. The estimate reflects patterns in a diabetes dataset,
not a specific clinical evaluation.

## Which indicators does the estimate use?

Sample placeholder content: the estimate uses gender, age, hypertension
status, heart disease history, smoking history, BMI, HbA1c level, and
blood glucose level. HbA1c expresses average blood sugar over recent weeks
as a percentage, while blood glucose is a point-in-time reading in mg/dL.
Higher BMI values reflect more body mass relative to height.

## Can the estimate replace a doctor?

Sample placeholder content: no. The estimate is informational only. Anyone
concerned about diabetes should discuss their readings with a qualified
healthcare professional. This sample policy text exists so that grounded
retrieval answers can be demonstrated end to end.
